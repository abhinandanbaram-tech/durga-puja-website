The Festival

Celebrating faith, culture, and belonging.

Durga Puja symbolizes the victory of good over evil, and the triumph of righteousness, courage, and compassion. It is a time of prayer, performance, art, and shared joy.

From the festive dhakis to the fragrant flowers and the illuminated pandals, every moment captures the heart of Bengal and beyond.
